## About me

I am an undergraduate Electrical Engineering student specializing in Control Systems, with a strong passion for control engineering, robotics, artificial intelligence, intelligent automation, and emerging technologies. My academic journey has allowed me to develop a solid foundation in electrical engineering principles while building a particular interest in the design, analysis, modeling, and implementation of modern control systems.

My primary interests lie in the development of intelligent and autonomous engineering systems that combine control theory with artificial intelligence, robotics, sensing technologies, and automation. I am particularly interested in robotic systems, industrial automation, robotic manipulators, intelligent motion control, autonomous systems, and AI-assisted control. I am fascinated by how advanced control algorithms and intelligent technologies can be used to make machines more accurate, efficient, adaptive, reliable, and capable of operating in complex environments.

As a Control Systems student, I am interested in both classical and modern control approaches, including feedback control, PID control, system modeling, simulation, stability analysis, and optimization. I am also interested in exploring how these techniques can be enhanced through artificial intelligence and machine learning to develop more adaptive and intelligent control systems. My goal is to understand not only the theoretical principles behind these technologies, but also how they can be implemented in real engineering applications.

Robotics is another major area of interest for me, particularly the application of robotic systems in industrial environments such as manufacturing and automotive production. I am interested in the control of robotic manipulators, trajectory planning, motion control, sensors, actuators, and the integration of AI into robotic systems. I am especially interested in multidisciplinary projects where control engineering can be combined with electrical power systems, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and robotics to develop complete engineering solutions.

Alongside my academic studies, I actively seek opportunities to expand my technical knowledge through research, academic projects, technical conferences, workshops, online learning, and independent study. I believe that continuous learning and practical experimentation are essential for developing as an engineer, particularly in rapidly evolving fields such as artificial intelligence, robotics, and automation.

I am also interested in engineering applications related to energy and electrical power, especially where power systems can be integrated with intelligent control and automation. I am interested in exploring technologies such as energy management systems, smart grids, renewable energy integration, electric vehicles, and intelligent energy systems, particularly when they involve advanced control and optimization techniques.

Through my academic projects and research interests, I aim to develop practical engineering solutions to real-world problems while strengthening my abilities in mathematical modeling, simulation, programming, system analysis, control design, and engineering problem-solving. I enjoy working on multidisciplinary challenges that require knowledge from different areas of electrical engineering and appreciate opportunities to collaborate with other students and researchers.

My long-term goal is to pursue advanced studies and research in areas related to control systems, robotics, artificial intelligence, and intelligent automation. I hope to contribute to innovative research and engineering projects that combine theoretical knowledge with practical applications and help advance intelligent technologies for modern industrial and engineering systems
